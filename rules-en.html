House of Chivalry

Alliance ranks,
responsibilities and standards

I. Purpose and principles

H0C is founded on teamwork, communication, mutual respect, determination and shared commitment to alliance success. Every member and Officer follows alliance rules, shares important information, supports alliance operations when needed, helps newer members and places H0C above personal gain.

No member or Officer stands above the alliance.

II. Officers

An Officer position is one of trust and responsibility, not privilege. Officers are active, accountable, cooperative, professional in conflict, discreet in internal matters and committed to the interests and reputation of H0C.

III–IX. Officer positions

Financial Advisor: oversees resources, investments and responsible use. Diplomatic Advisor: maintains professional external relationships, but cannot make major commitments without consultation. Warehouse Masters: organize resources and distribution. Defenders: coordinate defensive readiness and response. Lord Commander: plans strategy, rallies, reinforcements and major military events.

X. Major events and command

During organized operations, members follow the Lord Commander or designated event commander. Read instructions, follow objectives and priorities, report unavailable tasks, avoid actions that disrupt the operation, and do not put individual results above the alliance objective.

XI. Communication

Check announcements regularly, read event instructions, respond when Officers request information, report extended absences and ask questions when instructions are unclear. Officers communicate important information clearly, accurately and on time.

XII–XV. Member ranks and promotion

Baron → Count → Marquis → Officer. Promotion is based on activity, reliability, participation, teamwork, initiative, responsibility, communication, trust and contribution to H0C — not simply account strength. Higher rank means greater responsibility to the alliance.

XVI–XVIII. Absence, conflict and authority

Real life comes first; report longer absences whenever possible. Resolve disagreements respectfully and privately. No one may use rank for personal gain, resource abuse, intimidation, unauthorized diplomatic commitments or unnecessary conflict. Authority serves H0C and must be used fairly and transparently.

XIX. One Alliance — One Team

H0C succeeds when members act together. Every member has a role and every contribution matters.

H0C — One Alliance. One Team.
