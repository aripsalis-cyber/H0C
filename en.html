I. Purpose and principles

Honor in action.
Unity in purpose.

H0C ranks are not just titles. They represent trust, responsibility, activity and commitment to the life of the alliance.

Our strength depends not only on the strongest accounts, but also on our ability to cooperate, communicate effectively, participate in activities and support one another.

01

Respect

We treat H0C members and members of other alliances with respect.

02

Cooperation

We support rallies, reinforcements and operations whenever the situation requires it.

03

Responsibility

We share important information and follow alliance decisions.

04

Common Good

We place the interests of H0C above personal rank or personal benefit.

No member or Officer stands above the alliance.

II. Ranks and responsibility

Rank is
earned through trust.

Promotion is not a reward based solely on account strength. It reflects activity, reliability, participation, initiative and contribution to the development of H0C.

Baron→Count→Marquis→Officer

Member rank

Baron

An active member beginning the path toward greater involvement. Develops their account, learns procedures and supports alliance activities.

Member rank

Count

An active and engaged member who regularly participates in events and rallies and supports others.

Member rank

Marquis

A trusted leader among the members. Sets a good example, supports new players and is ready to take on additional responsibilities.

Position of trust

Officer

Helps the alliance operate effectively, protects its interests and maintains a positive and well-organized atmosphere.

Officer Positions

Financial Advisor

Oversees resources, investments and their responsible use for the benefit of the entire H0C alliance.

Diplomacy Advisor

Maintains professional relations with other alliances and protects the reputation of H0C.

Warehouse Master I and II

Organize alliance resources, their distribution and mutual backup in day-to-day operations.

Defender I and II

Coordinate defensive readiness, reinforcements and responses to threats.

Lord Commander

A strategist, organizer and communicator responsible for planning H0C military operations.

III. Military and events

One strategy.
One shared victory.

Lost RealmKvKSiege of WinterfellAlliance Conquest

During organized events, members follow the instructions of the Lord Commander or the designated event commander.

During operations, the goal is coordinated action rather than pursuing multiple competing strategies.

During major events

  • Read instructions and be available at the designated time whenever possible.
  • Follow the objectives and attack, rally and reinforcement priorities.
  • Inform the leadership if you cannot complete an assigned task.
  • Avoid independent actions that could disrupt the operation.

IV. Rules and communication

Clearly. Fairly.
With respect.

Communication

Check messages and announcements regularly. Ask questions when instructions are unclear and inform Officers about longer absences.

Absence

Private life comes first. If possible, inform an Officer about a longer absence so H0C can plan its activities.

Conflicts

We resolve misunderstandings privately, fairly and professionally. We avoid personal attacks and public arguments.

Use of authority

Ranks serve the alliance. Authority should be used fairly, transparently and in the best interests of H0C.

V. One Team

Every member has a role.
Every contribution matters.

We want to build an alliance that is organized, reliable, competitive, respectful and enjoyable to be part of.

H0C — One alliance. One team.
